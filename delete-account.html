Delete Your Belovia Account

This page explains how Belovia users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Delete your account from the app

If you can access your Belovia account, you can delete it directly from the app.

In the Belovia app:
  1. Open your profile.
  2. Open Account Management.
  3. Select Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot log in to Belovia, you can request account deletion by contacting our support team.

Email: belovia.support@gmail.com

Please include the email address or phone number associated with your Belovia account so that we can identify the account and process the request.

What will be deleted?

Account deletion is intended to remove your Belovia account and associated personal information, including your profile, connections, messages, and other account data.

Some information may be retained where required by law, necessary for security, or otherwise permitted by applicable requirements.

Privacy

For more information about how Belovia handles personal information, please see our Privacy Policy.

Support

If you have questions about account deletion, contact Belovia Support at belovia.support@gmail.com .
