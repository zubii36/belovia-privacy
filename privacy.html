Belovia Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Belovia respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Belovia collects, how that information is used, how it is protected, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated information.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a Belovia account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • County of origin
  • Current county
  • Preferred age range
  • Student or working status
  • Information you provide in your profile
  • Occupation
  • Relationship goal
  • Profile photograph, if provided

Belovia may also store information necessary to provide communication features, such as messages, connection requests, and images sent through the messaging feature.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by Belovia may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your profile to other Belovia users
  • Help users discover and connect with other users
  • Provide messaging and connection features
  • Maintain and improve the Belovia service
  • Protect the service against misuse and unauthorized activity
  • Respond to support requests

3. Information Sharing

Belovia does not sell your personal information.

Some information that you choose to include in your profile may be visible to other Belovia users because displaying profiles is part of the service.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of Belovia, its users, or others.

4. Messages and Profile Information

Information you voluntarily place in your profile may be visible to other users. Messages and images sent through Belovia's messaging features may be stored so that the messaging service can function.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information with other users through profiles or messages.

5. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Belovia account using the Delete My Account option available in your profile.

When an account is deleted, Belovia deletes the account and associated information stored for that account, subject to information that may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

6. Data Security

Belovia takes reasonable measures to protect information stored by the service against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Belovia retains account and service information for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

When an account is deleted, information associated with that account is deleted in accordance with the account deletion process, except where retention is required or permitted by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Belovia is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Belovia does not knowingly allow people under 18 to create accounts.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be made available through Belovia.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the Belovia support team through the contact information provided with the service.
